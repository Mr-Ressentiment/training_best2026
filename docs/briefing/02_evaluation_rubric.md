# Evaluation Rubric

Total: **100 points**

## 1. Problem & Impact — 20 points

Judges evaluate:
- relevance of the problem;
- clarity of the target user;
- severity/frequency of the pain point;
- usefulness of the proposed solution;
- potential measurable improvement.

Strong evidence:
- clear before/after workflow;
- believable target user;
- concrete time/cost/error reduction.

---

## 2. Innovation — 20 points

Judges evaluate:
- originality;
- creative use of AI;
- differentiation from existing tools;
- whether the solution is more than a generic chatbot.

Strong evidence:
- a product-specific workflow;
- orchestration across tools/data;
- novel interaction or automation loop.

---

## 3. Technical Implementation — 25 points

Judges evaluate:
- end-to-end functionality;
- architecture;
- robustness;
- sensible division between deterministic code and AI;
- quality of integration;
- demo reliability.

Strong evidence:
- working critical path;
- minimal fragile dependencies;
- graceful fallback behavior.

---

## 4. AI Quality & Trustworthiness — 15 points

Judges evaluate:
- grounding;
- hallucination mitigation;
- validation;
- uncertainty handling;
- transparency.

Strong evidence:
- citations or provenance;
- deterministic validation;
- explicit unknown/unavailable state;
- human approval where appropriate.

---

## 5. UX & Product Quality — 10 points

Judges evaluate:
- clarity;
- ease of use;
- speed to value;
- quality of the demo flow.

Strong evidence:
- user understands what to do without explanation;
- one obvious critical workflow;
- no unnecessary screens.

---

## 6. Pitch & Demo — 10 points

Judges evaluate:
- clarity;
- storytelling;
- timing;
- product demonstration;
- answers during Q&A.

Strong evidence:
- problem in 30 seconds;
- live demo early;
- architecture explained only after value is visible.

---

# Suggested Tie-Breakers

If two teams receive similar scores, judges should prefer the submission with:

1. More reliable end-to-end demo.
2. Stronger measurable user value.
3. Clearer justification for why AI is necessary.
4. Better handling of uncertainty and failure.
